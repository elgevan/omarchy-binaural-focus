## Generated audio

`assets/binaural-focus-40hz.opus` was generated specifically for this plugin from two independent mathematical sine waves:

- left channel: 134 Hz
- right channel: 174 Hz
- binaural difference: 40 Hz
- sample rate: 48 kHz stereo

It contains no recording, sample, melody, voice, or extracted media. Its complete generation recipe is committed at [`tools/generate-audio.sh`](tools/generate-audio.sh).
